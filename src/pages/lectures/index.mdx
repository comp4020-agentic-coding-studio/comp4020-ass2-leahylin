Lectures run **Mondays 10:00–12:00** in the Cold Storage Building (7), Room
2.11, and follow the same shape each week: the claim, the evidence behind it,
and the reading that carries it forward. The course builds cumulatively — each
week names the argument it inherits from the one before — so
[week 1](/lectures/week-01/) sets the pattern for the rest.

Lectures are recorded, but they're the smaller half of the course. The
[tutorials](/sessions/) are where the argument meets evidence from your own
kitchen — and that can't be caught on video.

The twelve lectures fall into four modules, and reading them in order is the
point:

- **Weeks 1–3 · Disenchantment** — cold as a manufactured commodity, from the
  ice trade to Freon, and the myth of modernity sold alongside it.
- **Weeks 4–6 · Micropolitics** — the interior as a jurisdiction, and the door
  as a stage.
- **Weeks 7–9 · The tyranny of time** — date labels, systemic waste, and the
  stuffed fridge as emotional solace. Where the argument becomes measurable.
- **Weeks 10–12 · Resistance and reconstruction** — surveillance capitalism in
  the kitchen, degrowth, and what could be built instead.

<Callout type="info">
  Most weeks' readings are listed below — but [week 1](/lectures/week-01/),
  [week 7](/lectures/week-07/) and [week 12](/lectures/week-12/) carry their
  own slide decks and are worth attending live: week 1 frames the whole
  course, week 7 turns the tyranny of time into something measurable, and
  week 12 — just fifteen minutes — is built to stay with you.
</Callout>
