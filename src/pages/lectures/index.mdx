Every lecture runs to the same shape: the week's claim stated and defended, an
outline of how it is built, the reading, and what to bring to the tutorial. Each
one also names the argument it inherits from the week before, so the semester
accumulates rather than resetting every Monday. [Week 1](/lectures/week-01/) is
the pattern.

Lectures run **Mondays 10:00–12:00** in the Cold Storage Building (7), Room
2.11, each teaching week of the semester. They are recorded and the recordings
appear on Cabinet — but the lecture is the smaller half of this course.
[The tutorials](/sessions/) are where the argument meets evidence from your own
kitchen, and those cannot be watched afterwards.

The twelve lectures fall into four modules, and reading them in order is the
point:

- **Weeks 1–3 · Disenchantment** — cold as a manufactured commodity, from the
  ice trade to Freon, and the myth of modernity sold alongside it.
- **Weeks 4–6 · Micropolitics** — the interior as a jurisdiction, and the door
  as a stage.
- **Weeks 7–9 · The tyranny of time** — date labels, systemic waste, and the
  stuffed fridge as emotional solace. Where the argument becomes measurable.
- **Weeks 10–12 · Resistance and reconstruction** — surveillance capitalism in
  the kitchen, degrowth, and what could be built instead.

<Callout type="info">
  This course runs in **Semester 1 2027**. Three lectures carry slide decks:
  [week 1](/lectures/week-01/), which has to introduce the course and make its
  argument in the same two hours; [week 7](/lectures/week-07/), on the tyranny
  of date labels, because that argument is mostly a matter of putting two
  labels side by side and watching a room realise it has never known the
  difference; and [week 12](/lectures/week-12/), which is fifteen minutes and
  built to stay that way. The other nine are delivered from the pages below,
  and the reading list is what you take away.
</Callout>
