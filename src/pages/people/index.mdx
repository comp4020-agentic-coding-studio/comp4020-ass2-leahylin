Reading a fridge as governed space is interpretive work. Establishing that it
produces waste is quantitative. An argument built on one without the other is
either unfalsifiable or uninterpreted, so the course is convened by two people.

- **[Simone Ravel](/people/simone-ravel/)** — weeks 1–6 and 10–12. The history,
  the theory, the ethnography and its ethics.
- **[Casper Nowak](/people/casper-nowak/)** — weeks 7–9, where the argument
  becomes measurable. Supervises the Counter-Fridge project.

Either of us will redirect you rather than send you away, so ask whoever seems
closest.
