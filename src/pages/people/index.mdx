This course sits between two methods that do not usually share a room. Reading a
fridge as governed space is interpretive work — material culture, classification,
performance. Establishing that the fridge produces waste is quantitative work —
audits, labelling studies, measurement. An argument built on only one of those is
either unfalsifiable or uninterpreted, so the course is convened by two people
and taught in two registers.

[Simone Ravel](/people/simone-ravel/) takes weeks 1–6 and 10–12: the history, the
theory, the ethnography and its ethics. [Casper Nowak](/people/casper-nowak/)
takes weeks 7–9, the module where the argument becomes measurable, and
supervises the Counter-Fridge project.

  <p>

    pick either — we will redirect you rather than send you away.
  </p>
</Callout>
