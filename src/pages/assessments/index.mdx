Each assessment takes one module and asks you to do something with it rather
than recall it. The [Fridge Ethnography](/assessments/fridge-ethnography/)
applies Module 2's micropolitics to a real household; the
[Critical Essay](/assessments/critical-essay/) works in Module 3, where the
argument becomes measurable; the
[Counter-Fridge](/assessments/counter-fridge/) answers Module 4 by proposing
something to build instead.

**Three of the four cannot be written from the library.** The ethnography needs
a household that consented to being observed, the essay is strongest when it
rests on your own audit data, and the design project grows out of the week 10
and 11 tutorials. Fieldwork can be borrowed in a way an argument cannot, so
submitting observations you did not make is treated as fabrication rather than
plagiarism — see [Policies](/policies/).
