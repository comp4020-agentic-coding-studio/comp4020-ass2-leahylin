**This week's question: the fridge now watches its own contents, and you — who is
that inventory really for?**

Inherited from week 9: the fridge sells reassurance. This week is about what it
has begun taking in exchange.

## Before the session

Read the Zuboff excerpts on the smart home.

Then list **three inferences** a vendor could draw from a fridge inventory that
are not about food. Do this before Wednesday rather than during — people find it
more unsettling when they arrive at it alone.

## In the session

The exercise is adversarial by design. Building the bad thing is a faster route to
understanding it than critiquing it from outside, and it is much harder to be
fooled later by a feature you have already designed yourself.

- **Design the dystopia** (40 min). In groups of four, specify **Smart Fridge
  2.0**, optimised for extraction rather than for the household. At least four
  features. Previous years have produced: locking high-calorie compartments based
  on heart-rate data; pushing weight-loss advertising after a late-night opening;
  auto-ordering with a supplier who paid for placement; sharing consumption data
  with a health insurer at renewal; and a "family wellness dashboard" that reports
  one household member's eating to another.

  For each feature, specify what it measures, what it infers, who receives the
  data, and what the business model is. A dystopia with no revenue model is not
  finished.

- **Pitching** (20 min). Each group pitches sincerely, in the register of a real
  product launch. The register matters — the discomfort of hearing it pitched
  well is the exercise.

- **Deconstruction** (30 min). Now take them apart:
  - **Which of your inventions already exist?** Usually about half, which is the
    moment the room goes quiet.
  - **Which would be legal** in your jurisdiction today?
  - **Which would a household actually accept**, if it were bundled with something
    genuinely useful?
  - Zuboff's frame: where is the behavioural surplus, and where is the lure?
  - Where is the **health discipline** — the point at which the appliance stops
    informing and starts governing?

## Afterwards

The [Final Project](/assessments/counter-fridge/) is open and due **Friday 28
May**. Today's exercise is its inverse: you have just built the maximally
exploitative version, so you now know precisely what a counter-design has to
refuse.

Speculative is allowed. Buildable is allowed. A reasoned refusal to build anything
is allowed, if you argue for it.
